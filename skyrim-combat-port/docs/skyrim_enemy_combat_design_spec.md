# Skyrim: combat design specification for enemy differentiation (v1.0)

**What this is:** a gameplay design handoff with concrete move kits, triggers, tells, cooldowns, counterplay, and differentiation targets for 42 enemy families. Another chat should not invent enemy behavior beyond this specification; it should flag feasibility issues rather than silently redesign.

**What this is not:** a Skyrim plugin, animation pack, finalized damage-balance spreadsheet, or tested release. Numerical timing values below are design targets to prototype; damage tuning awaits Skyrim testing, but move identity and decision loops are fixed.

## 1. Core rule: an ability must change the player's decision

Each enemy family's signature action has five fixed parts: (1) unique readable tell, (2) threat shape/range/status, (3) at least two counter options, (4) punish/recovery, (5) cooldown/anti-loop rule. A visible animation with no changed player response does not qualify. If players can use one tactic against spider, wolf, and bandit with no additional risk, those designs fail.

### Shared timing and control budget

- Quick ordinary attack tell: 0.45–0.65 s; signature/elite tell: 0.75–1.0 s; boss breath/large slam: 1.0–1.2 s. Must have both visual and audio cue (for accessibility, never rely on sound alone).
- After an attack commits, turning correction is limited; projectiles/charges do not home after launch.
- A signature may not repeat until its listed cooldown; never use the same signature twice consecutively. At most one major root/slow/stun effect can control the player at a time.
- Hard control target: standard stagger 0.25–0.45 s; knockdown/pin usually at most 0.7–0.8 s. Longer boss disable requires an explicit escape/counter. No stun-lock or forced multi-hit chain.
- Every signature must have at least two routes: e.g. interrupt/deflect vs dodge/cover; or resistance/spacing vs kill-priority. One exact build cannot be mandatory.
- Telegraphs identify what response is relevant: lane, cone, ground zone, melee arc, summon, or defensive feint. Generic red flash is not enough.

## 2. First three must-pass distinctions

### Spider vs wolf vs bandit: three different duels

| Family | Enemy's plan | Player's actual decision | Fail state to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frostbite spider | Control space with web; punish a slowed/near target with committed venomous leap/bite. | At range watch web aim and break line/dodge; at close range read hiss/crouch and sidestep fixed leap lane. Decide whether to clear web zone or pressure spider before next cycle. | Treating it as a wolf with poison slapped on; unavoidable web-root + leap chain. |
| Wolf | Win through pack geometry: one circles/feints, one makes a timed lunge. | Turn/position to stop flank, use area attack or bait one lunge and punish landing. | All wolves lunging simultaneously, or single wolf reusing spider's ranged slow. |
| Bandit | Win through role/tools: shield feint, archer sightline, potion, trap or caster windup. | Read equipment/role, break guard/line/cast, interrupt potion/trap or choose target priority. | All bandits using same melee loop with different gear/HP; enemies healing instantly. |

**Spider kit locked:** small spider hiss for 0.65 s, then committed leap OR bite; no web-snare. Large spider aims web 0.8 s, visible glob, slow 1.5 s; web cooldown 8 s; leaps only if player is close/slowed, with no midair tracking. Unique giant may use ceiling drop after scraping/hiss. This makes the spider control range/space; wolf pressures from flanks; bandit tests role response.

## 4. Dragon attack sequence (the requested animation/audio work)

1. **Bite:** jaw pullback, short growl; front-cone punish. Bite/grab only under existing low-health/killmove conditions; not a surprise one-shot.
2. **Front wing sweep (new):** dragon retracts shoulder/wing for 0.9 s, distinct cloth/air whoosh, then sweeps across frontal arc. Step out of arc or block at stamina cost. Miss recovery 1.2 s.
3. **Side wing buffet:** shorter tell (0.7 s), narrow side arc to clear the flank; not 360-degree hitbox.
4. **Tail sweep:** dragon rotates its torso first (0.8 s tell); rear arc only.
5. **Stomp/takeoff/landing:** local ground cue; low knockback, no random launch across the map.
6. **Breath:** neck/head rises, inhale and low rumble, mouth glow matches fire/frost, 1.2 s tell. Cone lasts up to 2.5 s; direction tracking slows after first 0.3 s and cannot snap-turn. Cover blocks the cone. Breath cooldown at least 8 s; recovery 1.5 s.
7. **Element variant:** frost adds one visible ice strip/slow; fire leaves a short burning strip; no elemental field silently overlaps the breath cone. Named dragons get exactly one extra signature (fan/ground ring/roar) and one phase cue, not all variant moves combined.

## 5. Rank/variant distribution rules

- Basic enemies get two ordinary attacks and at most one signature. Rank upgrades change one tactical property (better spacing, one added feint, or stronger tell/cooldown), not the entire move pool.
- Elite gets at most two signatures with distinct cooldowns; boss gets one phase change and at most two signature attacks unless a specific named design says otherwise.
- A faction role is assigned once per NPC: e.g. a bandit mage does not also receive alchemist, trapper and chief kits.
- Packs/squads share one controller rule: at most one wolf leap at a time; formation breaks when its anchor is interrupted; support NPCs cannot summon unlimited reinforcements.
- Difficulty presets may scale damage modestly, not erase telegraph time or counter windows. Do not solve difficulty through a large HP multiplier.
- DLC/Creation Club kits stay optional and isolated. If the content isn't present, base enemy lists are untouched.

## 6. Blind-playtest acceptance checklist

For each family, run the same-level/equipment 1v1 and a mixed encounter. Hide enemy labels in the first pass. A player should (a) describe the tell, (b) choose a changed response compared with spider/wolf/bandit baseline, and (c) name a second viable counter. Record deaths, hit chains, time spent controlled, signature repetition and whether sound/visual cue was noticed. If all three enemies are defeated by identical face-tank/light-attack behavior, redesign the attack loop; do not increase damage. Test both first person and third person, low/no sound, darkness, color-vision-safe VFX, and combat with two or more attackers.

## 7. State of handoff

Workbook `SK_Enemy_Port_Plan` now has exact move/cooldown and counterplay columns for all 42 archetype families; `FoA_Enemy_Inspirations` keeps source inspiration separate from invented Skyrim behavior. The Markdown document is the game-design source of truth. The earlier broad brief is retained for technical architecture only.

**No mod implementation is present.** Animations, sounds, AI behavior graphs, spells/records, plugin distribution and in-game tests remain future production tasks. The implementation chat should preserve the attack loops and ask about specific technical impossibilities; it should not make new design decisions silently.

## Поправка 22.11.2026: чем именно пробивается блок (решение пользователя)

Правило для щитоносцев заменяет прежнюю формулировку «shield feint → bash» на две ветки:

| Что делает игрок | Ответ врага | Обоснование |
|---|---|---|
| Блокирует **не щитом** (оружием, кулаками) | щитоносец выбивает игрока из блока **башем** | у блока без щита нет «жёсткой» стойки, баш её ломает |
| Блокирует **щитом** | щит пробивается **силовой атакой** того, у кого булава или двуручный молот | щит держит обычный удар, но не тяжёлое пробивание |
| Не блокирует | обычная атака/комбо, без баша | баш не должен быть неожиданностью вне блока |

Кулдаун на баш и на силовое пробивание — 7 с на одного врага. Реализация: `native/` (SKSE-плагин),
описание правил и логов — в `native/README.md`.
